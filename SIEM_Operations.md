# SIEM Operations

## Understanding SIEM Operations
SIEM operations refer to the daily activities performed by SOC teams to ensure that the SIEM platform functions effectively. These operations include reviewing alerts, analyzing false positives, tuning correlation rules, supporting investigations, and maintaining the system. Together, these tasks ensure that the SIEM continues to provide accurate, timely, and actionable security intelligence.

---

## Alert Review
Alert review is the process of examining alerts generated by the SIEM to determine their validity and severity.  
- Analysts triage alerts by checking event details, context, and potential impact.  
- The goal is to quickly distinguish between genuine threats and benign activity.  
- Effective alert review prevents analysts from being overwhelmed by noise and ensures that critical incidents are addressed promptly.  
- This step is often the first line of defense in SOC operations.  

---

## False Positive Analysis
False positive analysis involves identifying alerts that do not represent real threats.  
- SIEM systems may generate alerts for unusual but harmless activity.  
- Analysts investigate these alerts to confirm whether they are false positives.  
- Documenting false positives helps refine detection logic and reduce unnecessary alerts.  
- By minimizing false positives, SOC teams can focus on genuine incidents and improve efficiency.  

---

## Rule Tuning
Rule tuning is the process of adjusting correlation rules and detection logic to improve accuracy.  
- Analysts refine thresholds, conditions, and logic based on observed activity.  
- For example, a rule that triggers on “five failed logins” may be tuned to “ten failed logins within one minute” to reduce noise.  
- Rule tuning ensures that SIEM adapts to the organization’s environment and evolving threat landscape.  
- Continuous tuning is essential for balancing sensitivity and precision in detection.  

---

## Investigation Support
Investigation support refers to the tools and processes that assist analysts in exploring alerts and incidents.  
- SIEM provides search capabilities, timelines, and visualization features to trace suspicious activity.  
- Analysts use these tools to identify compromised accounts, track attacker movement, and confirm data exfiltration.  
- Investigation support ensures that alerts are validated and escalated into incidents when necessary.  
- This stage is critical for building evidence and supporting incident response.  

---

## Operational Maintenance
Operational maintenance ensures that the SIEM platform remains reliable and effective.  
- Tasks include updating correlation rules, integrating new log sources, and applying software patches.  
- Maintenance also involves managing storage capacity, retention policies, and system performance.  
- Regular health checks prevent downtime and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.  
- Operational maintenance keeps the SIEM aligned with organizational needs and evolving security challenges.  

---

## Key Takeaway
SIEM operations are the backbone of daily SOC activities. By performing alert review, false positive analysis, rule tuning, investigation support, and operational maintenance, organizations ensure that their SIEM platform remains accurate, efficient, and resilient against modern cyber threats.
